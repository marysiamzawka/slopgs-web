# Demo song credits

All six are unmodified downloads from [Battle of the Bits](https://battleofthebits.com/), tagged "MSGS MIDI" there -- each was written with the Microsoft GS Wavetable Synth's sound specifically in mind, which is exactly what this player reproduces. All are **CC BY-NC-SA 3.0** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/3.0/), confirmed against the license badge shown on each entry's own page.

- **`vonotron-assembler.mid`** -- "Vonotron Assembler" by Strobe, Winter Chip XIV, Dec 27 2018. https://battleofthebits.com/barracks/Entry/View/30336/
- **`nyrmodian-bird-park.mid`** -- "Nyrmodian Bird Park" by pigdevil2010, Winter Chip XIII, Jan 27 2018. https://battleofthebits.com/barracks/Entry/View/27317/
- **`general-serum.mid`** -- "~ GENERAL SERUM ~" by Kot and A64, Spring Tracks XII, May 11 2023 (idea contributions from Abstract, per the track's own notes). https://battleofthebits.com/barracks/Entry/View/60184/
- **`transcendental.mid`** -- "transcendental" by A64, Summer Chip XIV, Jul 26 2024. https://battleofthebits.com/barracks/Entry/View/69940/
- **`midian-city-nightclub.mid`** -- "Midian City Nightclub" by Strobe, Summer Chip III, Jun 27 2013. https://battleofthebits.com/barracks/Entry/View/10285/
- **`domestic-droid-rights-foundation.mid`** -- "Domestic Droid Rights Foundation" by Strobe, Winter Chip VI, Feb 6 2011. https://battleofthebits.com/barracks/Entry/View/5026/
